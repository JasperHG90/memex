# Acme Corp — Engineering Update (March 2023)

**Date:** March 12, 2023

In Q1 2023, Acme Corp's engineering organization grew from 15 to 20 engineers.
The new hires joined the platform team and the data infrastructure team.
The leadership planned to double the headcount over the next 18 months.
