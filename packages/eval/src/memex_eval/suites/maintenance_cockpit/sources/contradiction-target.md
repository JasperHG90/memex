# Deployment Cadence Policy

The deployment cadence is weekly on Tuesdays. All production releases go through
the staging environment first. The release train departs at 10:00 UTC every
Tuesday, and hotfixes outside this window require VP-level approval.

Teams must submit their merge requests by Monday 17:00 UTC to be included in the
next Tuesday release. Late submissions are automatically deferred to the
following week.
