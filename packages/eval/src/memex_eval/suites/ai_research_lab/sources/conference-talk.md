# NeurIPS 2025 — Keynote Summary

**Speaker:** Elena Vasquez (AI Research Lab)
**Title:** "Scaling Transformer Architectures for Real-World NLP"

## Talk Summary

Elena Vasquez delivered a 45-minute keynote on transformer scaling techniques for
production NLP systems. Key contributions discussed:

1. A novel sparse-attention mechanism that reduces FLOPs by 60% with minimal
   accuracy loss on benchmarks.
2. The AI Research Lab's pipeline for continuously fine-tuning models on
   domain-specific corpora.
3. Collaboration with Raj Mehta on embedding-model improvements.

## Q&A

Several attendees asked about hardware requirements; Elena Vasquez referenced
the lab's recent A100 GPU expansion led by Lisa Chang.
