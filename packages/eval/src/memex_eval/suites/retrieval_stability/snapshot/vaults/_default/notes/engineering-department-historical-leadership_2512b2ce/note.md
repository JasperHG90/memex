# Engineering Department — TechCo Global (Historical Leadership)

**Former Department Head:** Alex Chen
**Tenure:** January 2020 to December 2023

Alex Chen led the Engineering Department at TechCo Global from January 2020
through December 2023, overseeing the initial cloud migration and establishing
the team's core practices. During his tenure from 2020 to 2023, Alex built the
department from 12 to 35 engineers before handing over to Ruby Martinez at the
end of December 2023.
