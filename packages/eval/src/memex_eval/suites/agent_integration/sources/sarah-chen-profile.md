# Sarah Chen — Profile

**Title:** Engineering Manager, Project Alpha
**Joined:** January 2024
**Reports to:** CTO

## Role

Sarah Chen leads the Project Alpha team — currently five engineers
plus rotating SRE coverage. She owns delivery for both Phase 1 (API +
data pipeline, shipped) and Phase 2 (analytics surface, in flight).

## Operational responsibility

Sarah was on-call lead during the August 2025 Redis incident; she
facilitated the Q3 retrospective and owns the in-process cache
migration.

## Contributions outside her team

- Proposed and championed the ruff line-length 100 convention
  (Q1 2025 style review).
- Proposed the type-stub-first policy for new modules after the
  March 2025 post-incident review.

## Background

Prior to Acme, Sarah was a staff engineer at two payments companies.
She has 12 years of Python production experience and a track record
of operating PostgreSQL at scale.
